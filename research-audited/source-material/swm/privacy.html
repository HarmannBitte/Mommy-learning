Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Study With Mommy isn’t open yet. For now, this site is a waitlist, and our Instagram account sends people the link to it. This page says, in plain words, what those keep about you, why, who else handles it, how long it’s kept, and how to have it deleted. It describes what the site does today. When the app opens, it will have a privacy policy of its own.

The short version

  • To join the waitlist we need your email address. We also note roughly where you joined from and what brought you, but never your IP address.
  • We use it to tell you about your seat. We don’t sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.
  • Every email from us has a link to leave the list, which deletes your address.
  • This site sets no cookies of its own, and runs no analytics scripts, ads or trackers.
  • Our Instagram bot doesn’t keep what you wrote, your username or your account id.

The details

When you join the waitlist

Your email address, what you’re studying if you tell us, and roughly where you joined from. Never your IP address, name or age.

We keep:

  • Your email address, in lower case.
  • What you’re studying, if you type it. It’s optional. We read it to choose which subjects to support first, and it’s never shown on the site. If you join again and type something new, it replaces the old.
  • When you joined.
  • Which platform’s link brought you, if the link named Instagram or TikTok (like studywithmommy.com/?from=instagram). Nothing else in the link is kept.
  • Roughly where you were: the country, region and city that Cloudflare, our host, matches to the network address you joined from. We don’t keep the address itself, and the city is often just the nearest large one.
  • What brought you: if you joined inside an app’s own browser, which app (like TikTok or Instagram), going by what your browser says it is; and if another site linked you to us, only that site’s name (like tiktok.com), never the page.
  • If you join again, we keep the first link, place and app we noted.
  • Whether your confirmation email went, and if it didn’t, a short error code.
  • Which of our emails we’ve sent you, and whether each went, so nobody gets the same one twice.
  • If we invite you to test the app early and you ask to: what you told us (what you’re studying, an exam, your phone or laptop, how often you’d use it, that you’re 18 or over), Cloudflare’s guess at your country, and whether we said yes. It goes when you leave the list.
  • When we first invited you in, once the app opens, so each invitation goes to you only once.
  • For the invitation, which version we sent you and the course it pointed to, chosen from what you said you’re studying. The email never repeats your words. If your address looks misspelt (like gmail.con), we may correct it before we write.
  • If you make an account, we note that it came from the waitlist, and which address on it, with the account.

That’s all. We don’t ask your name or your age, there’s no account or password, and nothing else about your visit is kept with your entry. We use where people joined from to see which posts and places the waitlist reaches. Joining again with the same address doesn’t add a second entry. The Mommy you choose on the page stays in your browser and isn’t sent to us.

We keep your address because you asked us to, by joining. You can change your mind at any time by leaving the list. If someone else typed your address, the confirmation email says so, and its link takes it off straight away.

Only our small team can see the list: in our Cloudflare account, and on our own admin page, which needs a one-time sign-in code or the team’s access key. We can download a copy of it to work with, and we delete those copies when we’re done.

The emails we send

One confirmation, then only news about your seat. No tracking, and a link to leave in every one.

When you join, we send you one confirmation, from welcome@support.studywithmommy.com. After that, we’ll only email you about your seat, from mommy@support.studywithmommy.com.

Our emails are sent by Cloudflare, or by Resend when Cloudflare can’t, so they receive your address and what each email says, in order to deliver it, and keep a record of what they sent for a while, under their own privacy policies. Our emails have no tracking pixels, and we don’t track whether you open them or click their links.

Every email has a “Leave the list” link at the bottom, and many mail apps show it as an unsubscribe button too. The link asks you to confirm, then deletes your address and the record of what we sent you, straight away. It carries your address and a signature, so it only works for you; anyone you forward the email to could use it.

If you reply to one, your reply comes to us. Replies, and anything you email us, reach us through Resend too. About your information, write to info@studywithmommy.com.

Our Instagram bot

It sends the waitlist link when you say “MOMMY”, and keeps none of your words, your username or your account id.

If you comment on one of our posts, or send our Instagram account a message, with the word “MOMMY” in it, our bot sends you the waitlist link in a private message. For a comment, it also replies “Sent you the link 💌” under it, so you know to look.

To do that, Instagram (Meta) sends the bot every comment on our posts and every message to our account, with the sender’s account id so it can reply, and for a comment their username. The bot reads each one once to look for the word, and saves none of it. For each one with the word in it, it keeps only:

  • the id Instagram gave that comment or message,
  • whether it was a comment or a message, and when it came,
  • whether the link was sent, and an error code if it wasn’t.

It doesn’t keep what you wrote, your username or your account’s id, though Instagram’s id for a comment or message could be matched to it on Instagram while it’s there. These records stop the bot answering the same comment twice and tell us how many links it has sent, and we keep them while the bot runs. The bot also keeps the sign-in token for our own Instagram account, which isn’t about you.

The link the bot sends ends in ?from=instagram, so if you then join the waitlist, we’ll know Instagram brought you (see above). Your comment or message itself stays on Instagram, under Instagram’s privacy policy, and you can delete it there. The bot’s private reply starts a conversation with you in our account’s messages, which our team can read in Instagram, as with any message sent to any account.

Visiting the site

Hosted by Cloudflare, with no analytics, ads or trackers.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles each request to it, which includes your IP address, what your browser says it is, and the page asked for. What we look at from Cloudflare is totals, like how many times a day the front page was loaded, not who loaded it.

  • We use no analytics scripts, ads, tracking pixels or social media buttons. Our pages are served so that Cloudflare can’t add its own analytics to them, and they load nothing from any other site: even the fonts are ours.
  • Our servers log errors, not visits: what kind of error, and where in our code. We don’t log your email address, your IP address or anything you typed. Those logs last a few days, a week at most.
  • To stop anyone flooding the waitlist, we count sign-ups from each network address, an hour at a time. Each count is kept under a scrambled code made from the address, never the address itself, and is deleted within two days.
  • Cloudflare asks browsers to report connection failures to it, to keep its network working.

Cookies and your browser

No cookies of our own. Your browser remembers which Mommy you chose.

This site sets no cookies of its own. Cloudflare may set a short-lived security cookie if it needs to check that a visitor isn’t a bot. Your browser keeps at most two small things for the site, on your device:

  • which Mommy you chose, so she’s still chosen next time you visit (in local storage, until you clear it). It never leaves your device;
  • if you join from an Instagram or TikTok link, which of the two it was, for that tab only (in session storage, gone when you close the tab).

Your browser’s settings can clear both.

Who else handles it

Cloudflare, Resend and Meta, each only to do its job. We don’t sell your information.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the bot, stores our database, and sends our emails.
  • Resend sends our emails when Cloudflare can’t, and receives the ones you send us.
  • Meta runs Instagram, where the bot works.

Cloudflare and Resend handle your information only to provide their services to us, under their own privacy policies, and may do so in the United States or elsewhere. What you do on Instagram, following our link from it included, is under Instagram’s privacy policy. We don’t sell your information or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

Your entry until you leave the list or the waitlist closes, and how long everything else lasts.
  • Your waitlist entry, and the record of emails sent to it: until you leave the list or ask us to delete it. Once we’ve told you your seat is ready and the waitlist closes, we’ll delete it.
  • The bot’s records: while the bot runs.
  • Copies of the list we download: until we’re done with them.
  • Sign-up counts: two days at most.
  • Error logs: a week at most.
  • Backups. Our database keeps backups that can restore deleted data for up to 30 days, so anything deleted is gone from those within 30 days too.

Children

Not directed to children under 13, and how a parent can have their details deleted.

Study With Mommy is made for students, and isn’t directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13, and if we learn we have, we delete it. We don’t ask anyone’s age, though.

If you’re a parent or guardian and think your child under 13 has joined the waitlist, use the “Leave the list” link in any email we’ve sent to that address, which deletes it straight away, or email info@studywithmommy.com with the address and we’ll delete it for you. If they commented or messaged on Instagram, the comment or message can be deleted on Instagram, and if you email us their username, we’ll delete the bot’s record of it and our side of the conversation.

Deleting your information

  • The waitlist: use the “Leave the list” link at the bottom of any email from us. It deletes your address and the record of what we sent you, straight away. Or email info@studywithmommy.com with the address, and we’ll delete it within 30 days.
  • Instagram: delete your comment or message on Instagram. The bot keeps only Instagram’s id for it, with no words, username or account id. If you’d like that record deleted too, or our side of the conversation the bot started, email info@studywithmommy.com with your Instagram username and we’ll find and delete them.
  • Anything else, or to ask what we hold about you, or to correct it: email info@studywithmommy.com. We’ll answer within 30 days.

Contact

For anything about your information or this page, email info@studywithmommy.com.

Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we’ll update this page and its date first. If a change affects the address you gave us, we’ll email you before it applies to you.
