How we’ll study

  1. Bring me your slides

    Lecture slides, a textbook chapter, class notes, even a photo of the page you scribbled on. They stay private, and you can delete them whenever you like.

  2. I’ll draw it out for you

    Every idea becomes a short lesson. The diagram builds on the board while I explain it, one step at a time, and I always say which slide it came from.

    From your slides: lecture 4, slide 12

    At the top of the curve, the slope is zero.

  3. Stop me any time

    We’re on a call the whole lesson. Ask out loud, type it, or circle the part that’s confusing, and I’ll pause and explain just that.
